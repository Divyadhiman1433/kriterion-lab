# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and academically honest project environment. Participation is open regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive review feedback.
- Credit datasets, methods, code, and prior work accurately.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive information.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and limitations in experimental conclusions.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate misrepresentation of results, plagiarism, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the security contact documented in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation when conduct conflicts with this policy.
